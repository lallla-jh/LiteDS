# Privacy Policy — LiteDS

**Last updated: 2026-05-23**

LiteDS ("the App") is a free, open-source Nintendo DS emulator for Android developed by lallla-jh.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

LiteDS does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

- Name, email address, or any account information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Payment or financial information

---

## Data Stored Locally

All data created by the App (save files, save states, settings, ROM paths) is stored **only on your device** and is never uploaded to any server.

---

## Third-Party Services

LiteDS does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

---

## Permissions

The App requests the following permissions solely to provide core emulator functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_*` | Read ROM files from device storage |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` | Keep emulation running while the app is in the foreground |
| `VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback for on-screen controls |

No permission is used for data collection or advertising purposes.

---

## Children's Privacy

LiteDS does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The App contains no user accounts, online features, or data collection of any kind.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
